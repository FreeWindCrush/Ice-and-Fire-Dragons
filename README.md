# Ice-and-Fire-Dragons
Ice and Fire: Dragons — the definitive dragon mod for Minecraft. Hunt Fire, Ice, and Lightning dragons, steal their eggs, hatch and ride your own. Plus Hippogryphs, Sea Serpents, Gorgons, Cyclops, and a full mythical bestiary. Works with Forge and NeoForge on 1.18.2–1.21.1. Requires Citadel.
