## 📖 What Is Ice and Fire: Dragons?

**Ice and Fire: Dragons** is a large-scale fantasy mod that transforms Minecraft's dragons from a single boss encounter into a full progression system. Wild dragons patrol the world. Dragon nests hide precious loot and eggs. And with enough effort, you can hatch, tame, ride, and armor your own dragon companion.

The mod also introduces dozens of mythological creatures and structures, making the entire world feel more dangerous, mystical, and rewarding to explore.

**Key highlights:**
- ✅ **Three dragon species** — Fire, Ice, and Lightning with unique breath attacks

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: What is Ice and Fire: Dragons?**
A: A large-scale dragon mod that adds three dragon species, taming, riding, dragon gear, and dozens of mythical creatures.

**Q: Is Ice and Fire free?**
A: Yes — 100% free.

**Q: What Minecraft versions are supported?**
A: **1.18.2, 1.19.2, 1.20.1, and 1.21.1**. Community NeoForge ports exist for newer versions.

**Q: Forge, NeoForge, or Fabric?**
A: Forge and NeoForge are official. A community Fabric port exists.

**Q: What does it require?**
A: **Citadel** is required. For 1.12.2 and older, LLibrary was used instead.

**Q: How do I get a dragon?**
A: Slay a female Stage 4–5 dragon for a chance at an egg. Hatch it by placing it in fire (Fire), water (Ice), or rain (Lightning).

**Q: How long does it take to raise a dragon?**
A: Each growth stage takes about 25 in-game days. Full growth takes roughly 125 days. Use Dragon Meal to speed it up.

**Q: Can I ride a dragon?**
A: Yes — from Stage 3 onward.

**Q: Can I armor my dragon?**
A: Yes — Dragon Armor can be equipped for extra protection.

**Q: What other creatures are there?**
A: Hippogryphs, Sea Serpents, Gorgons, Cyclops, Pixies, Trolls, Hydras, Myrmex, and more.

**Q: Does it work on Bedrock / mobile?**
A: No — **Java Edition only**.

**Q: Can I use it with other mods?**
A: Yes — compatible with JEI, Tinkers' Construct, HWYLA, and others.

**Q: How do I uninstall it?**
A: Delete the `.jar` files from `.minecraft/mods/` and restart.
